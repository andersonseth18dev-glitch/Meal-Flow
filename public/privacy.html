Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026 · Operated by Seth Anderson, Arizona, USA

The short version: We collect only what we need to run the service. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties except to process payments and host the service. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Anderson Heirloom Recipes collects, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. By using our service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you sign up
  • Diet preferences: Dietary filters you select such as Keto, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free etc.
  • Recipes: Recipes you add to your personal vault, including ingredients, steps, photos, and notes
  • Meal plans: Your weekly meal planning data
  • Community content: Recipes you choose to share to the community page and your display name preference
  • Family information: Your family name and which accounts are linked under your family plan

Information collected automatically:

  • Device and browser information: Browser type, operating system, and device type
  • IP addresses: Logged automatically by our hosting provider Netlify
  • Session data: Authentication tokens stored in your browser to keep you signed in
  • Usage data: Which features you use, how often you visit, and general interaction patterns

Payment information:

All payment processing is handled by Stripe. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card number, expiration date, or CVV. We only receive confirmation that a payment was successful and your Stripe customer ID for managing your subscription. You can review Stripe's privacy practices at stripe.com/privacy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide the recipe, meal planning, and grocery list features
  • Process your subscription and manage billing
  • Send in-app notifications about your account, family activity, and community updates
  • Display your shared recipes on the community page according to your display name preference
  • Review community recipe submissions for compliance with our content guidelines
  • Improve the service based on how it is used
  • Respond to support requests and legal inquiries

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Supabase: Our database provider stores your account data, recipes, and meal plans. Data is stored on secure servers. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Stripe: Processes your payments. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • Netlify: Hosts the website and serverless functions. See netlify.com/privacy.
  • Anthropic Claude API: Used to screen community recipe submissions for content compliance. Recipe content is submitted for review but is not stored by Anthropic beyond the immediate request. See anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Unsplash: Recipe names are used to search for food photography. No personal data is shared. See unsplash.com/privacy.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Community Content and Visibility

When you share a recipe to the community page, other paid subscribers can view it. You choose how your name appears — as your family name, your first name only, or anonymously. Your email address is never displayed publicly. Recipes in your private family vault are only visible to members of your family plan and are never shared without your explicit action.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, your account and data remain stored so you can resubscribe and pick up where you left off. If you request account deletion, we will permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, with the exception of records we are required to retain for legal or financial compliance purposes (such as payment records).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct your data: Update inaccurate information through your profile settings
  • Delete your data: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your data: Request your recipe data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent: Cancel your subscription and stop using the service at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at andersonseth18dev@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Anderson Heirloom Recipes uses browser cookies and local storage for:

  • Keeping you signed in between visits (authentication session)
  • Remembering your preferences
  • Temporary data during account setup (cleared immediately after use)

We do not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.

8. Children's Privacy

Anderson Heirloom Recipes is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will promptly delete the account and associated data.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure password hashing through Supabase Auth, and row-level security policies that ensure users can only access their own data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify you through the app. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
